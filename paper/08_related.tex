\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Triggered and retroactive capture} Beyond the flight recorders of
\autoref{sec:tools}, deciding after the fact what to keep is an old idea:
DTrace aggregations and speculative tracing~\cite{cantrill2004dtrace},
LTTng~\cite{lttng}, rtla~\cite{rtla}, and Hindsight's retroactive sampling
for distributed systems~\cite{zhang2023hindsight}. \sys adopts the pattern;
what it adds is a record of the frame's causes, computed in the kernel, in
place of a raw window.

\paragraph{Wait and critical-path analysis} The analyses of
\autoref{sec:offcpu} follow waits precisely but offline, over a full trace,
or sum them over a whole run, where a rare hitch's cause ranks far below the
top (\autoref{sec:eval-catch}). \sys executes a bounded, online version of
the same analysis, scoped to one frame and run only when the frame goes
bad.

\paragraph{Accounting per unit of work} VarMRI accumulates kernel and
hardware events per server request and emits one record per request, using
a kernel patch~\cite{varmri}. \sys applies per-unit accounting to frames,
as delay accounting and PSI do per task and system-wide, adds the waker and
the chain, and runs as verified BPF on a stock kernel.

\paragraph{Frame-aware profilers} Nsight Systems delimits Vulkan frames at the
present call and flags actions whose per-frame time far exceeds a rolling
median~\cite{nsight}; Perfetto's FrameTimeline classifies jank on
Android~\cite{perfetto-frametimeline} and AOSP relies on a blocked-reason
tracepoint that upstream kernels lack~\cite{aosp-blocked-reason}; hwui\_mon
and frame-analyzer detect Android frames with
uprobes~\cite{hwui-mon,frame-analyzer}; Tracy~\cite{tracy} and
Superluminal~\cite{superluminal} need instrumentation or full captures;
PresentMon and MangoHud measure frame times without
causes~\cite{presentmon,mangohud}. None of them attributes frames to kernel
causes online.

\paragraph{Linux gaming tooling} systing and VaporMark (\autoref{sec:tools})
serve sched\_ext developers~\cite{scx}. \sys is complementary: its records
are compact enough to leave running for a session and could be exported
into those tools' formats.
