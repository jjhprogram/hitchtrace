\section{Conclusion}

This paper presented \sys, which explains frame hitches in unmodified Linux
games by partitioning the presenting thread's frame by kernel cause,
resolving its waits online through wake chains carried in task-local
storage, and deciding in the kernel, at frame end, whether the frame is
worth a record. Its records were right on injected stalls and on
reproduced stutters, and on rare hitches it alone attributed nearly every
one and named its culprit, at a fraction of the output of the tools that
held the same frames, for the cost of an ordinary scheduler tracer. The
case studies also say plainly what a record cannot yet tell: that a sleep
was late, which CPU a thread waited for, and who else was using the disk.
